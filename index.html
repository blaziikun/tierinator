Export

PNG is an ordinary picture for sharing. Sprites are embedded at the chosen detail so you can zoom in without blur. Pixel sprites are scaled up with hard pixel edges. Dream World art stays true vector in SVG. Hovering a sprite in the SVG shows its name, and the PDF is searchable by name even with labels off.

Forms as separate Pokémon

Ticked groups get their own entry to rank. Sprite styles (shiny, back, GO, costumes) always stay with their Pokémon. Split or merge single forms by right-clicking a Pokémon.

Variants

Versions of the same character (Dragonoid → Delta → Neo…, Four Arms in every series). Separate: each is its own entry to rank. Combined: one entry, and its pictures include every variant. To group items yourself, select them on the tier list and press Group.

Tierinator

Tier lists for anything. Free, open source, no ads, no accounts.

Your data

Your lists are kept only in this browser. There's no account, no tracking, no cookies and no analytics. Nothing you rank is sent anywhere unless you share a link.

Some features fetch from other sites straight from your browser (sprites, searches, wikis, extra fonts), so those sites see your IP address as if you'd visited them. The full list is on the privacy page.

Getting started

  1. Tierinator starts empty. Add items to make a list, the list menu (top left) for a Pokémon list or a template, or open a saved list or backup with File → Load…
  2. Drag pictures into tiers, or click a few and press 1–9. On a phone: tap to select, then tap a tier label.
  3. Quick rank (above the pool, or Q) shows unranked items one at a time, big.
  4. Right-click (long-press on a phone) any item to change its picture, tag it or add a note.
  5. Add items: upload, type a list, search anime / films / music, or pull a whole wiki category.
  6. ⇅ on a tier sorts it head-to-head ("this or that?").
  7. Variants: versions of one character (every Dragonoid, every Four Arms) can be separate entries or combined into one, like Pokémon forms.
  8. Lists live in this browser. Now and then use File → Back up all lists.
